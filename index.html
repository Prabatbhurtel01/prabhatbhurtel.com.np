01 About

A disciplined creative
building systems,
brands & bold work.

I'm Prabhat Bhurtel — entrepreneur, business development manager, web designer, and BSc IT student, all at once. I didn't wait to "figure it out" before starting — I built Everlyco Digital from zero while still in university, learned to sell before I learned to design well, and started closing real clients while most people my age were still picking a major. Most people choose a lane. I built the intersection.

On the business side, I've worked as a Business Development Officer, closing deals and managing client relationships professionally — then took that exact skill set and built Everlyco Digital's entire outreach and acquisition system from the ground up. Every website in my portfolio, I designed and built myself. No templates, no outsourcing. I operate like a strategist first and a technician second: systems over hustle, positioning over noise, results over vanity metrics.

Before I was building an agency, I was a YouTuber and video editor — I grew a channel past 5M+ views and edited for 10+ clients at once, which is where I actually learned what makes people stop scrolling and start trusting. Today I bring that same instinct into content creation, while building a parallel skill set in data and business analytics — because the agencies that win the next decade won't just create, they'll measure. That's the intersection I'm building my career around.
